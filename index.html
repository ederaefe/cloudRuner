TRIPLE-STAGE NITRO OVERCHARGE
Stage 1 is standard cruise (140-200 km/h). Holding Boost engages Stage 2 Afterburner (250 km/h). When your nitro gauge enters the flashing yellow threshold (>70%), tap Boost again to latch Stage 3 Hyper-Overdrive (320 km/h) with maximum FOV elasticity and radial speed warp!
SNAP AILERON ROLLS (+22% NITRO)
Execute a 360 corkscrew by pressing Q or E. The drone's collision volume compresses by 45%, allowing you to slip through narrow gaps between skyscrapers and instantly replenishing +22% Nitro.
KNIFE-EDGE FLIGHT (CONTINUOUS SCORE & NITRO)
Hold C to bank 90 degrees with automatic counter-rudder compensation. Slice through vertical skyscraper canyons and crane legs to stream continuous bonus score multipliers and continuous Nitro recharge.
PUGACHEV'S COBRA AIRBRAKE (62% SPEED DUMP)
Press X to perform an authentic aerodynamic post-stall pitch flare. Your wing planform acts as a massive high-drag airbrake, dumping 62% forward speed in 0.5 seconds to negotiate sharp hairpin entries without crashing into barriers.
NEAR-MISS FACADE BUZZING
Skim building facades, crane gantries, or rival drones within 3.5 meters at speeds above 100 km/h. Triggers a directional sonic air-crack, camera buffeting, and immediate +14% Nitro surge.
AI WINGMAN ESCORT (TEAMWORK SQUAD)
In Teamwork Squad mode, AI Wingman ECHO-01 flies formation escort with you. Stay within the 25-meter energy tether beam to receive continuous high-rate Nitro replenishment.
CARGO EXTRACTION OPS
In Cargo Extraction modes, hover over rooftop payload crates to latch the magnetic winch cable. Carry the crate back to base helipads and descend below 36 meters to score points before time expires.
